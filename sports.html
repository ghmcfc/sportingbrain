Sport guides

Not "the ball is round". How each sport actually works, what the experts are watching for, and the people who defined it.

American football

Chess with collisions: four attempts to gain ten yards, again and again.

🏃
Athletics

Run, jump, throw. The simplest sport there is, measured to the hundredth.

🏀
Basketball

Ten players, a 24-second shot clock and a scoring run every few minutes.

🥊
Boxing

The hardest sport to get good at, and the easiest to understand.

🏏
Cricket

A game of patience, pressure and very precise maths, in three very different formats.

🥌
Curling

Chess on ice, with sweeping.

🚴
Cycling

An individual sport that is won by teams, and decided by the wind and the mountains.

🎯
Darts

Three darts, a board 2.37m away, and the maths has to be done in your head.

⚽
Football

Eleven against eleven, and the cleverest game is the one you don't see on the highlights.

🏎️
Formula 1

The fastest racing on earth, decided as much in the garage and the pit lane as on track.

⛳
Golf

The fewest shots wins, and the hardest opponent is the course.

🏑
Hockey

Fast, skilful and played with a stick that only has one usable side.

🏇
Horse racing

Speed, stamina and form, on the Flat and over jumps.

🏑
Hurling

Ireland's ancient game, and possibly the fastest field sport in the world.

🤼
Kabaddi

A contact sport of tag and wrestling, played in one breath.

🏐
Netball

Fast, physical and tactical, with no running with the ball and a three-second rule.

Rugby league

Thirteen a side, six tackles, and nowhere to hide.

🏉
Rugby union

Fifteen-a-side, and much of it is a fight for the ball you can barely see.

🦶
Sepak takraw

Volleyball, but with feet, knees, chest and head. The overhead kicks are absurd.

🎱
Snooker

Geometry under pressure, and a 147 is the perfect frame.

🟡
Squash

Two players in a glass box, and the fittest usually wins.

🏊
Swimming

Four strokes, eight lanes and a lot of hundredths.

🎾
Tennis

A one-on-one fight where the scoring system is designed to produce drama.
