Irish sporting moments

Ar an Lá Seo

24 February 2007

England at Croke Park

With rugby played at Croke Park for the first time, Ireland beat England 43-13 in one of the most emotional days in Irish sport.

17 March 2018

Grand Slam on St Patrick's Day

Ireland beat England 24-15 at Twickenham to complete their third Grand Slam.

21 March 2009

O'Gara's drop goal in Cardiff

Ronan O'Gara's late drop goal gave Ireland a 17-15 win over Wales and their first Grand Slam since 1948.

10 April 2021

Blackmore makes history

Rachael Blackmore won the Grand National on Minella Times, the first woman to win the race.

13 April 2025

McIlroy's Grand Slam

Rory McIlroy won the Masters in a play-off to complete the career Grand Slam.

12 June 1988

Who put the ball in the English net?

Ray Houghton's header after six minutes in Stuttgart beat England 1-0 at Euro 88. Ireland's first match at a major tournament became a national holiday in all but name.

18 June 1994

Giants Stadium

Ray Houghton's chip beat Italy 1-0 in New Jersey at the start of the 1994 World Cup.

25 June 1990

The nation holds its breath

Packie Bonner saved from Daniel Timofte, then David O'Leary scored the decisive penalty in Genoa. Ireland were in the World Cup quarter-finals.

21 July 2019

Lowry's Open at Portrush

Shane Lowry won the Open by six shots at Royal Portrush, the first Open on the island since 1951.

26 July 1987

Roche in yellow on the Champs-Élysées

Stephen Roche won the Tour de France on the way to the Giro, Tour and world title treble.

28 July 2024

Armagh again

Twenty-two years on, Armagh beat Galway to win their second All-Ireland football title.

29 July 2021

The O'Donovan-McCarthy double

Paul O'Donovan and Fintan McCarthy won Ireland's first Olympic rowing gold, in the lightweight double sculls.

30 July 2024

Wiffen's gold

Daniel Wiffen won the Olympic 800m freestyle in Paris, the first Irish man to win an Olympic swimming medal.

8 August 2021

Harrington's Tokyo gold

Kellie Harrington won Olympic lightweight gold in Tokyo, three years before doing it again in Paris.

9 August 2012

Taylor's gold

Katie Taylor beat Sofya Ochigava in the London 2012 lightweight final, in the first Olympic women's boxing tournament.

19 August 2018

Limerick end the wait

Limerick beat Galway to win the All-Ireland hurling title for the first time since 1973.

19 September 1982

Darby's goal

Seamus Darby's late goal for Offaly stopped Kerry's five-in-a-row. Not every Kerry fan has forgiven him yet.

22 September 2002

Armagh's first Sam

Armagh beat Kerry to win their first All-Ireland football title.

28 September 2013

Clare's replay hat-trick

Shane O'Donnell scored three first-half goals as Clare beat Cork in the replayed All-Ireland hurling final.

11 October 2022

Barrett at Hampden

Amber Barrett's goal against Scotland sent the Republic of Ireland women to their first World Cup.

5 November 2016

Chicago

After 111 years of trying, Ireland beat the All Blacks, 40-29 at Soldier Field.

1 December 1956

Delany's Melbourne gold

Ronnie Delany came from ninth place on the last lap to win the Olympic 1500m, then knelt on the track in thanks.

What's in the Irish edition

Faoi

SportingBrain Ireland is the Irish edition of SportingBrain, the free daily sports site. It has 263 hand-written questions on Irish sport across 8 sports, all 32 counties of the island, 36 Irish sporting greats, a weekly Irish sports book and plain-English guides to Gaelic football and hurling, and it grows every week. The games change at midnight, every day.

We cover the island as a whole, the way most of its sports are run: GAA, rugby, hockey and boxing all play as one Ireland. So Joey Dunlop and Mary Peters sit alongside Katie Taylor and Henry Shefflin. Women's sport gets the same space as men's: camogie, ladies' football, the Irish women's rugby and football teams, and Rachael Blackmore, Kellie Harrington and Cora Staunton.

Facts are checked when we write them and settled ones only: nothing that changes week to week. If you spot something wrong, tell us and we will fix it.
