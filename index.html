Free daily sports quizzes, games and stories

SportingBrain is a free sports site for people who know their sport. Daily games built on real matches, hard quiz questions, and true stories from every sport, men's and women's. No sign-up, no betting, ever.

Today's games

  • Call It: Cricket: A real over from a real final. Call the runs and wickets before the ball is bowled.
  • Call It: Football: A moment from a great final. Who scores next, and how many more?
  • Connections: Sport: Sixteen names, four hidden groups. One a day.
  • Riddlink: Football: Three linked cryptic football clues, eight guesses. New every day.
  • Who Am I?: Five clues, hardest first. Name the sporting great.
  • Ground Guesser: A real venue, zoomed right in. Name it in as few steps as you can.
  • Sports Grid: Nine squares, nine guesses. Name the club that fits the row and the column.
  • Beat the Brain: Five hard questions a day, Hard or Expert. The Brain has already had a go.

Quizzes

  • Name Them All: Every Ballon d'Or winner, every PDC champion. Type them all before the clock stops.
  • Weekly Expert: Ten expert questions. New every Monday. For people who win pub quizzes.
  • Pub and Club Quiz: Four rounds of ten, answer sheet and host notes. Free to print and run.
  • Countdown Clock: The big events coming up, ticking down, with a quick quiz for each.
  • Grandparent Rewind: A round for every decade from the 1950s. Play it with someone who was there.
  • Pick a Sport: Ten questions on the sport you know best. A fresh set every time.
  • Higher or Lower: Which number is bigger? Keep the run going.
  • True or False: Sporting facts and fibs.
  • Speed Round: Sixty seconds. As many as you can.
  • Puzzle archive: Missed a day? Every Beat the Brain is here.
  • Family challenge: Pass the phone round. Who knows the most?

Games

  • Is It a Foul?: Eighteen real rules scenarios across eight sports. Fair or foul?
  • Stat Attack: Top trumps against the Brain with Grand Slam legends. Pick your stat.
  • Stats Detective: A batting line from a World Cup final. Whose was it?
  • Pick Your XI: Any player, any theme: Greatest, Duffers, Daft Names, Cult Heroes. Share it and get it rated.
  • Matchday Bingo: A card for the next game you watch. Tap squares as they happen, or print it.

Family and seasonal

  • Sporting Advent Calendar: A door a day from 1 December, each with a question and a story.
  • Ground Hopper Passport: Stamp every ground and venue you visit. Kept on your device only.
  • Sports Day at Home: Eight events for the garden or the park, a scorecard and a 30-day challenge.

SportingBrain Ireland

  • Irish Legends: Five clues to one of Ireland's sporting greats, hardest first.
  • Pick a Sport: Ireland: Ten questions on one Irish sport: GAA, rugby, soccer, racing, boxing and more.

  • 53,000 at Goodison, then a 50-year ban: The biggest crowd for a women's club match stood for almost a century. Within a year, the FA had shut women out of every ground it controlled.
  • The 26th lap: An Ethiopian 20-year-old and a white South African ran the 10,000 metres together. Then they ran one more lap, hand in hand.
  • The worst Olympic marathon ever run: One well for water. A winner who arrived by car. A champion kept going on rat poison. St Louis, 1904.
  • 555 and then Toulouse: For five and a half years nobody beat Jahangir Khan. Not once. Then a New Zealander who had lost to him 30 times walked on court in France.
  • Wimbledon champion at 15, in a corset: Lottie Dod won Wimbledon five times, then went off and won the British golf title, played hockey for England and took an Olympic medal in archery.
  • The first Cricket World Cup was a women's one: Two years before the men got round to it, England's women won a World Cup at Edgbaston, paid for by a millionaire and driven by a captain who would not take no for an answer.
