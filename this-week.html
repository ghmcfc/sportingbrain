The stories that mattered

Football

Spurs win 3-1 at Manchester City in the WSL

Tottenham beat Manchester City 3-1 on Sunday at City's home ground. A day earlier, London City Lionesses beat Crystal Palace 6-2, equalling Chelsea's 6-2 win over Spurs in October as the highest-scoring WSL game of the season.

Source: Sky Sports

Football

Pochettino's first game ends 4-0 at Anfield

Manchester United's new manager could hardly have had a worse start. Leny Yoro was sent off for a professional foul after eight minutes, Dominik Szoboszlai scored from the free kick, and Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Víctor Muñoz added the rest. United stay in the relegation zone.

Source: FotMob

Rugby union

Ireland beat the world champions, who still reach the final

Robert Baloucoune scored twice as Ireland held on to beat South Africa 20-17 in Dublin. It was the Springboks' second defeat in a row, the first time that has happened since 2022, and they were without coach Rassie Erasmus, who is serving a six-match ban. They still go through to the first Nations Championship final, at Twickenham on Sunday.

Source: Sky Sports

Formula 1

Verstappen wins in Las Vegas, three weeks after the title was settled

Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday night. The championship itself was decided on 1 November in Mexico City, where Kimi Antonelli became the youngest world champion in Formula 1 history, aged 20 years and 68 days.

Source: Al Jazeera

Rugby union

The All Blacks face their worst year of the professional era

New Zealand lost again at the weekend. Their national broadcaster says they are now on the brink of the worst year in the professional era.

Source: RNZ
