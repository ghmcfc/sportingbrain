For parents and carers

What the Kids Zone is, and how we keep it safe.

What it is

Five sport questions a day in two age bands (6 to 8 and 9 to 12), true facts, and activities to do at home or in the garden. Every question is checked against reliable sources before it goes up, the same as the rest of SportingBrain.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no account and no form. If your child types their name on the certificate, it stays in the browser on your device and is never sent to us. We don't use tracking cookies anywhere on SportingBrain.

No ads, no chat

The Kids Zone carries no advertising, now or in the future. There is no chat, no comments and no way to contact other users. There are no links to betting, ever, anywhere on the site.

The UK Children's Code

We follow the Information Commissioner's Age Appropriate Design Code: high privacy by default, no profiling, no nudges to share data, and no features designed to keep children on screen. Each visit is five questions, then we suggest something active to do instead.

The activities

The "try this at home" ideas are simple and low-risk, but please supervise younger children, choose a clear space away from roads, stairs and windows, and use soft balls indoors.

The newsletter

Our email newsletter is for over-13s only. If you'd like it, please sign up yourself.
