Amazing facts

Lottie Dod won Wimbledon in 1887 when she was only 15, and nobody younger has ever won it.
The fastest tennis serves have been measured at over 160mph.
In 1920, 53,000 people watched a women's football match at Goodison Park in Liverpool.
A Formula 1 car has to be weighed with its driver after the race to check it isn't too light.
The first Cricket World Cup was a women's one, in 1973, two years before the men's.
Jahangir Khan won 555 squash matches in a row and didn't lose for over five years.

Try this at home

Be a commentator

Watch two minutes of any match with the sound off and commentate it yourself. Record it and play it back.

Make your own stats sheet

Pick a game you play with friends and keep score: goals, saves, passes. Who's top after a week?

Reaction test

Ask someone to drop a ruler between your fingers without warning. Catch it as fast as you can and read the number. Lower is quicker.

Design a kit

Draw a new kit for your favourite team, or invent a team of your own, with a name and a badge.
