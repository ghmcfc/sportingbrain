Amazing facts

The first footballs were sometimes made from a pig's bladder, blown up and covered in leather.
Usain Bolt ran 100 metres in 9.58 seconds. That's faster than most people can cycle.
A cricket ball is made of cork and string, wrapped in leather and stitched by hand.
Tennis balls used at Wimbledon are kept in fridges so they bounce the same every time.
The Olympic rings are blue, yellow, black, green and red.
Golf balls have hundreds of little dents called dimples, which help them fly further.

Try this at home

Keepy-uppy challenge

How many times can you keep a soft ball off the ground with your feet? Try again tomorrow and beat your score.

Balance like a gymnast

Stand on one leg with your arms out. Count how long you can hold it. Now try the other leg.

Target throw

Put a bucket a few big steps away and throw rolled-up socks into it. Step back one step each time you score.

Animal races

Race a grown-up doing bunny hops, crab walks and bear crawls. Play on grass or carpet, away from stairs.
