How to play

Your ball. A teammate sends it in and a ring closes on the sliotar. Act as it meets the inner circle.

  • Catch is safe. Then strike before you're hooked: Point has the wide white target, Goal the narrow yellow one.
  • Hit first time. Perfect timing can rattle the net; scrappy timing goes wide.
  • Hand pass keeps the move going and makes the targets bigger, but chains can be intercepted.
  • Ground balls can only be struck. Pick it up with your hand and it's a free out.

Their ball. A forward runs at you. Tap the left and right sides to step across and stay in front of him: the ring under you turns green when you're covering him. Then tap Tackle as the timing ring closes.

  • If he shoots while you're in front, your tackle is a block.
  • If he tries to go round you, it's a hook.
  • If a long ball comes in, get under the yellow marker and it's an intercept.
  • Dive in too early and he skips past you. Perfect timing can win a point on the counter.

Shootout. Tap a spot in the goal, then strike as the needle crosses the bar. In goal, watch the taker and tap where you'll dive. Keys 1, 2, 3 and Space work too.
