Sport guide

Hockey

Fast, skilful and played with a stick that only has one usable side.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of eleven use sticks to hit, push or flick a ball into the opponents' goal. Only the flat side of the stick can be used.

Goals can only be scored from inside the shooting circle, the D-shaped area in front of goal.

Penalty corners are a key set piece: the attacking side injects the ball from the backline and the defenders sprint out from behind the goal line.

Scoring

One goal for every ball into the net from inside the circle.

Formats

  • Four quarters of 15 minutes.
  • Indoor hockey.

What the experts watch for

  1. Penalty corner routines: drag flicks and deflections.
  2. Aerials: lifted passes over the top of the press.
  3. Rolling substitutions: changes happen constantly.

The greats

Teun de Nooijer

Three-time world player of the year with the Netherlands.

Luciana Aymar

Eight-time world player of the year with Argentina.

Kate Richardson-Walsh

Captained Great Britain to Olympic gold in 2016.

Talk like an expert

Tap a term to see what it means.

Drag flick

A powerful, lifted shot used at penalty corners.

Reverse stick

Using the flat side of the stick on your left.

Aerial

A ball lifted high over the defence.

Green card

A two-minute suspension.

Shootout

A one-on-one decider, started from 23m out.

When it's on
  • Every four years: Olympics and World Cup.
  • Season-long: FIH Pro League.
