Sport guide

Hurling

Ireland's ancient game, and possibly the fastest field sport in the world.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of 15 use a wooden stick (hurley) to strike a small ball (sliotar). Players can catch the ball, and carry it on the stick.

You can only hold the ball in your hand for four steps; then you must bounce it on the stick or pass.

The women's version is camogie, governed by the Camogie Association.

Scoring

A goal (under the bar) is three points. A point (over the bar between the posts) is one.

Formats

  • Inter-county championship for the Liam MacCarthy Cup.
  • Club championships.

What the experts watch for

  1. Puck-outs: the goalkeeper's restarts are now tactical set plays.
  2. Striking off either side.
  3. Catching the high ball under pressure.

The greats

Henry Shefflin

Ten All-Ireland titles with Kilkenny.

Christy Ring

Eight All-Ireland titles with Cork.

Angela Downey

Twelve All-Ireland camogie titles with Kilkenny.
