Kabaddi

Where it's played: India, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Iran and more

A raider crosses into the other team's half, tags as many opponents as possible, and gets back before being tackled, all on a single breath, chanting 'kabaddi, kabaddi' to prove it. Seven players a side are on court.

Did you know? Kabaddi is thousands of years old and comes from India.
