Darts

Three darts, a board 2.37m away, and the maths has to be done in your head.

Photo: Dartboard. Santeri Viinamäki / Wikimedia Commons

How it works

Players start at 501 and take turns throwing three darts to score points down to exactly zero. You must finish on a double (or the bullseye, which counts as double 25).

The board is split into 20 numbered segments, each with a thin double ring around the outside and a treble ring halfway in. Treble 20 (60) is the highest single dart.

The top players average around 100 per three darts. The difference between winning and losing is usually the doubles.

Scoring

Score down from 501. Highest three-dart score 180. Highest checkout 170 (treble 20, treble 20, bull). The nine-dart finish is the perfect leg.

Formats

  • Sets and legs: the PDC World Championship is played in sets (best of five legs).
  • Legs only: most other tournaments.

What the experts watch for

  1. Three-dart average: above 100 is elite.
  2. Checkout percentage: how often a player hits the double when they have a chance.
  3. Setting up: leaving a preferred double, such as 32 (double 16), so a miss still leaves another double.

The greats

Phil Taylor

16 world titles.

Michael van Gerwen

Three PDC world titles and a long spell as world number one.

Fallon Sherrock

The first woman to win a match at the PDC World Championship, in 2019.

Luke Littler

Won the PDC world title in 2025 aged 17.

Eric Bristow

Five world titles and the man who made darts a TV sport.

Ton-80

A score of 180.

Shanghai

Single, double and treble of the same number in one visit.

Bed and breakfast

A score of 26: single 20, single 5, single 1.

Madhouse

Double 1, the last resort.

Nine-darter

A perfect leg of 501 in nine darts.
