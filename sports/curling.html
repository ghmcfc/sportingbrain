Sport guide

Curling

Chess on ice, with sweeping.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of four take turns sliding granite stones towards a target called the house. Each team throws eight stones per end.

Sweepers brush the ice in front of the stone to make it travel further and straighter.

Tactics revolve around the hammer, the advantage of throwing the last stone of an end.

Scoring

After each end, the team with the stone closest to the centre scores a point for each of its stones closer than the opponent's nearest stone.

Formats

  • Fours.
  • Mixed doubles, an Olympic event since 2018.

What the experts watch for

  1. The hammer: teams with the last stone want to score two or more.
  2. Guards: stones placed in front of the house to protect others.
  3. Takeouts: knocking opponents' stones out of play.

The greats
