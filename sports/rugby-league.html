Rugby league

Thirteen a side, six tackles, and nowhere to hide.

Photo: Twickenham Stadium. Diliff / Wikimedia Commons

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of 13. The attacking side has six tackles to score or gain ground. After the sixth, the ball goes to the other side.

After each tackle the tackled player gets up and rolls the ball back with his or her foot: the play-the-ball. There are no rucks or lineouts.

Teams usually kick on the last tackle to gain ground or try to score, which is why the fifth and sixth tackles matter so much.

Scoring

Try 4 points, conversion 2, penalty goal 2, drop goal 1. A drop goal from 40 metres or more in some competitions is worth 2.

Formats

  • Super League in Europe, with the Grand Final at Old Trafford.
  • The NRL in Australia and New Zealand, and the State of Origin series.
  • The Challenge Cup, final at Wembley.
  • The Women's Super League and the NRLW.
  • A World Cup for men, women and wheelchair rugby league.

What the experts watch for

  1. Set completion: how many sets a team finishes without losing the ball.
  2. The ruck speed: how fast the play-the-ball happens decides how stretched the defence is.
  3. The last-tackle kick: a grubber, a bomb or a 40/20.

The greats

Wally Lewis

The King of Queensland, the face of State of Origin in the 1980s.

Ellery Hanley

Captain of Wigan and Great Britain, and a coach after.

Sam Burgess

Leader of South Sydney's 2014 title win, playing with a broken cheekbone.

Jessica Sergis

A star of the NRLW and Australia's Jillaroos.

Talk like an expert

Tap a term to see what it means.

Set

Six tackles in a row for one team.

Play-the-ball

How play restarts after a tackle.

40/20

A kick from your own half that bounces out inside the opponent's 20: you get the ball back.

Golden point

Extra time where the next score wins.

Dummy-half

The player who picks up from the play-the-ball.

When it's on
  • Spring: Challenge Cup final at Wembley.
  • Summer: State of Origin in Australia.
  • October: Super League Grand Final at Old Trafford.
