Sport guide

American football

Chess with collisions: four attempts to gain ten yards, again and again.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of 11. The attacking side (the offense) has four plays, called downs, to move the ball ten yards. Make it and they get four more.

Each play starts from a set position and stops when the ball carrier is tackled or a pass falls incomplete, so every play is planned in advance.

Teams use separate units for attack, defence and kicking, so squads are huge: 53 players in the NFL.

Scoring

Touchdown 6 points, then an extra point kick (1) or a two-point try. Field goal 3. Safety (tackling the ball carrier in his own end zone) 2.

Formats

  • NFL: 32 teams, a 17-game regular season, play-offs, then the Super Bowl in February.
  • College football in the USA, with crowds of over 100,000.
  • The NFL plays regular-season games in London most years.

What the experts watch for

  1. Third down: the play that decides whether a drive lives or dies.
  2. The quarterback reading the defence before the snap and changing the play.
  3. The battle in the trenches: the offensive and defensive lines.

The greats

Tom Brady

Seven Super Bowl wins, more than any franchise.

Jerry Rice

The NFL records for career receptions, receiving yards and touchdowns.

Lawrence Taylor

Changed how defences rush the quarterback.

Walter Payton

For years the NFL's career rushing leader.

Talk like an expert

Tap a term to see what it means.

Down

One attempt to move the ball. Four per set.

Snap

The ball being passed back to start a play.

Red zone

Inside the opponent's 20-yard line.

Sack

Tackling the quarterback behind the line before he throws.

Interception

A pass caught by the defence.
