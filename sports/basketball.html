Basketball

Ten players, a 24-second shot clock and a scoring run every few minutes.

Photo: Oracle Arena, Oakland. BrokenSphere / Wikimedia Commons

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of five try to shoot the ball through a hoop 10 feet (3.05m) high. You must dribble to move with the ball.

The shot clock forces a team to shoot within 24 seconds. That pace means momentum swings fast.

Modern basketball is driven by the three-point shot. Teams spread the floor with shooters to create space to drive to the rim.

Scoring

Two points for a shot inside the arc, three from beyond it, one for a free throw.

Formats

  • NBA and WNBA games: four quarters, 12 minutes (NBA) or 10 minutes (WNBA and FIBA).
  • 3x3 basketball, an Olympic sport since 2020.

What the experts watch for

  1. Pick and roll: the most common play in the game.
  2. Spacing: shooters in the corners stretch the defence.
  3. Rebounding: second chances often decide close games.

The greats

Michael Jordan

Six NBA titles and six Finals MVPs.

LeBron James

The NBA's all-time leading scorer.

Diana Taurasi

The WNBA's all-time leading scorer and multiple Olympic golds.

Bill Russell

11 NBA titles in 13 seasons.

Lisa Leslie

The first WNBA player to dunk in a game, in 2002.

Talk like an expert

Tap a term to see what it means.

Triple-double

Ten or more in three categories, such as points, rebounds and assists.

And-one

A basket scored while being fouled, with a free throw to come.

Paint

The rectangular area near the basket.

Fast break

Attacking before the defence is set.

Zone defence

Guarding areas instead of players.

When it's on
  • October to June: NBA season.
  • May to October: WNBA season.
