Sport guide

Netball

Fast, physical and tactical, with no running with the ball and a three-second rule.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of seven. Each player wears a bib showing their position, and each position is restricted to certain thirds of the court.

You can't run with the ball, and you must pass or shoot within three seconds. Defenders must stay at least 0.9m (three feet) from the player with the ball.

Only the Goal Shooter and Goal Attack can score, and only from inside the goal circle. The game is about moving the ball fast through the thirds and creating a clean feed into the circle.

Scoring

One point per goal. In Super Shot periods in some leagues, goals from the outer part of the circle score two.

Formats

  • Four quarters of 15 minutes.
  • Fast5: a shorter, five-a-side variant.

What the experts watch for

  1. Centre-pass conversion: the percentage of their own centre passes a team scores from. The best convert well over 80 per cent.
  2. Gains: intercepts and deflections that win the ball back.
  3. Feeding into the circle: the space the Wing Attack and Goal Attack create for the shooter.
  4. Shooting percentages under pressure.

The greats

Irene van Dyk

Over 200 caps across South Africa and New Zealand.

Laura Geitz

Australian captain who won world and Commonwealth titles.

Jhaniele Fowler-Nembhard

Jamaican shooter with remarkable accuracy and volume.

Geva Mentor

England's long-serving defender and a 2018 Commonwealth gold medallist.

Talk like an expert

Tap a term to see what it means.

Footwork

The rule about which foot you can move once you've caught the ball.

Contact

Touching an opponent so as to affect their play. A penalty.

Obstruction

Defending closer than 0.9m to the player with the ball.

Centre pass

The restart after every goal, alternating between teams.

Held ball

Holding the ball for more than three seconds.

When it's on
  • Every four years: Netball World Cup.
  • Every four years: Commonwealth Games (England won gold in 2018).
  • Spring and summer: Netball Super League in the UK.
