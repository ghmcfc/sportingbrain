Sepak takraw

Where it's played: Southeast Asia, especially Thailand and Malaysia

Think volleyball, but you can't use your hands. Two teams of three keep a small woven ball in the air with their feet, knees, chest and head, and send it over a net. The star move is the spike: a player leaps up and kicks the ball over from above head height, often flipping right over as they do it.

Did you know? The ball is traditionally woven from rattan, a kind of palm.
