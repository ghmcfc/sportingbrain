Sepak takraw

Volleyball, but with feet, knees, chest and head. The overhead kicks are absurd.

How it worksWhat to watch forThe greatsTalk like an expertWhen it's onGet involved

How it works

Two teams of three play over a net about 1.5m high, using a rattan or plastic ball. Hands are not allowed.

Each side gets three touches to return the ball. The spike, an overhead kick known as the roll spike, is the signature move.

Scoring

Rally scoring to 21, best of three sets.

Formats

  • Regu (three-a-side).
  • Doubles and quad events.

What the experts watch for

  1. The roll spike, a scissor kick over the net.
  2. The serve, kicked from a circle near the back.

The greats

Thailand's national teams

Have dominated Asian Games competition for decades.
