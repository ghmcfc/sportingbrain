About SportingBrain

SportingBrain is a free daily sports hub for every fan, every sport and every age. Football gets you through the door; everything else, from netball to darts, F1 to kabaddi, keeps you coming back. Women's sport sits front and centre, right alongside the men's game.

The Brain is our own cartoon mascot. He sets the puzzles, plays against you, and is a terrible loser.

No betting. No gambling ads. Ever.

Privacy, in plain English

What we collect: nothing about you. You don't need an account to play.

What stays on your device: your scores, streak and font choice are saved in your own browser so they're there next time. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Cookies and adverts: there are none yet. Before we add any, we'll ask your permission first, and there will never be targeted adverts aimed at children.

For parents: SportingBrain is built to be safe for families. There's no chat, no messaging between users and no sign-up needed to play.

Got a fact wrong?

We check our facts carefully, but if you spot a mistake, please tell us and we'll fix it.
