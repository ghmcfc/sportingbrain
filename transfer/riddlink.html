How to play

  1. Three five-letter words, linked: the last letter of each answer is the first letter of the next. Crack all three.
  2. Every clue is cryptic: one part defines the answer, the rest is wordplay, such as an anagram ("rearranged", "shuffled"), a hidden word ("found in"), sounds-like, or two meanings at once.
  3. Every answer comes from the working world: projects, procurement, finance, sales and tech delivery. When you finish, you get what each word means.
  4. The week gets harder: Monday and Tuesday are gentle (wordplay type shown free), Wednesday and Sunday are medium, Thursday to Saturday are tough.
  5. The tiles show how close you are:
    A
    U
    D
    I
    T
    Slate (with a dot): right spot. Amber (with a ring): in the word, wrong spot. Grey: not in the word.
  6. You get 8 guesses for all three words. Stuck? Each word has three helps: the wordplay type (1 guess), a letter (1 guess) or a plain clue (2 guesses). Guesses left over are your score.
