Sports Day at Home

Eight events you can run in a garden, a park or a hallway, with things you already own. Print the scorecard, pick teams and go. Then try the 30-day challenge.

The events

  1. Egg and spoon race. Use a potato or a ball instead of an egg. Ten big steps there and back.
  2. Sock basketball. Roll up socks and throw them into a laundry basket from three distances. 1, 2 and 3 points.
  3. Long jump on the grass. Jump from a line with both feet together. Measure with shoes, heel to toe.
  4. Keepy-uppy. Best of three tries. Under-8s can let it bounce once between touches.
  5. Balance challenge. Stand on one leg with eyes shut. Longest time wins.
  6. Obstacle course. Cushions to step on, a chair to go round, a towel to crawl under. Time each run.
  7. Target throw. Throw a soft ball at a cardboard box from five big steps. Five tries each.
  8. Relay. Teams pass a rolled-up sock as the baton. Run to the tree and back.

Grown-ups: pick a safe, clear space and keep an eye on younger ones. Points idea: 3 for first, 2 for second, 1 for everyone who takes part.
